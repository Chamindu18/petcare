# Owner notifications (Spark)

Staff appointment confirmation and rejection, queue check-in and consultation transitions, and adoption-provider decisions create owner in-app notifications in the same Firestore transaction as the source change. Rules check the source document before and after the write, actor authorization, recipient, target, type, message and server timestamp. Deterministic IDs prevent a second notification for the same transition.

Staff cancellation notifications are blocked because current appointment rules allow only the appointment owner to cancel. A staff cancellation notification would require a separately reviewed, authorized cancellation workflow. Owner cancellation does not notify the owner.

Android local reminders use inexact scheduling. Confirmed appointments are scheduled 24 hours before their start when that time is still ahead. Vaccination reminders use the recorded next due calendar date, normally at 09:00 the day before. The notification preference and Android permission gate scheduling. Firestore snapshots update or cancel reminders when records change; logout and account switching clear the prior owner's reminders. A record entered after its preferred vaccination reminder time but before the due day gets a near-term reminder.

The Android manifest includes the plugin's scheduling and reboot receivers, and the app uses an IANA device timezone. Exact alarm permission is intentionally absent. Android may deliver inexact alarms late, and device power policies can delay or suppress them. Delivery after process termination and reboot has not been verified on a device. The app should be opened to reconcile data after offline changes or a timezone change.

The in-app event text uses canonical generic wording so Firestore Rules can validate it without trusting sender-supplied names or medical details. Appointment time, queue number and adoption listing name are copied from related records and checked by rules. The notification screen routes appointment/queue events to My Appointments and adoption decisions to My Adoption Requests. Pet and hospital names can be viewed on the owner screens; they are not copied into notification text because the event writers do not have a single rules-verifiable snapshot of those display names.
